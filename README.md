## 🎮 About the Game

ELDEN RING is a dark-fantasy action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and released on February 25, 2022. It combines open-world exploration, challenging real-time combat, character progression, and extensive build customization. The Tarnished Pack released on August 28, 2026, adding two starting classes, new armor sets, and Torrent appearance customization.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ELDEN RING Tarnished Pack**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and gameplay adjustments for the PC version of ELDEN RING with the Tarnished Pack installed. It can modify player resources, rune handling, combat conditions, movement, and inventory behavior while preserving the game's core systems, including HP, FP, stamina, equipment load, status effects, Sites of Grace, Torrent, and fast travel.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for PC players who want to experiment with builds, explore the open world more freely, reduce repeated resource farming, practice boss encounters, or revisit the base game and Shadow of the Erdtree with customized difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents the player's HP from decreasing during exploration and combat.
- **Unlimited FP** — Keeps Focus Points full for repeated spellcasting, Ashes of War, and spirit-related abilities.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Removes normal stamina limits for attacks, guarding, sprinting, jumping, and evasive actions.
- **No Rune Loss** — Protects carried runes after death so they do not need to be recovered from the death location.
- **Rune Multiplier** — Adjusts the number of runes received from defeated enemies and other valid rune sources.
- **Ignore Equipment Load** — Disables the normal effect of carried weight on movement and dodge speed.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Consumables** — Allows supported consumable items to be used without reducing their stored quantity.
- **Easy Crafting** — Removes or reduces material requirements when creating supported craftable items.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Kills** — Causes supported attacks to defeat ordinary enemies with a single successful hit.
- **Infinite Poise** — Prevents incoming attacks from interrupting the player's actions through normal poise damage.
- **No Status Buildup** — Prevents harmful status meters such as bleed, frost, poison, scarlet rot, madness, and sleep from filling.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Modifies outgoing damage for weapons, spells, skills, and other supported attacks.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts walking, sprinting, jumping, and mounted movement speed.

### 🌍 World

- **Teleport to Waypoint** — Moves the player to a selected map location or waypoint for faster travel across the Lands Between and the Realm of Shadow.
- **Freeze World Time** — Pauses the normal passage of time while leaving the player free to explore and fight.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement-related options after loading into the game. Movement speed adjustments apply to the player and may affect sprinting, jumping, dodging, and mounted travel. Use moderate values near cliffs, elevators, narrow platforms, and scripted areas. Teleport options should be used away from active combat and preferably near a Site of Grace or another stable location.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory or storage menu before changing item quantities or crafting requirements. Use unlimited consumables for arrows, bolts, throwing items, healing items, and other supported resources. Avoid editing quest-critical objects, key items, remembrance rewards, or multiplayer-related data. Save normally after confirming that the intended quantity is displayed.

### World & Environment

World options affect exploration across the Lands Between and the Realm of Shadow. Fast-travel through the in-game map remains the safest way to move between discovered Sites of Grace. Teleport carefully when quests, elevators, dungeons, or boss arenas are active, since compatibility normal loading transitions can place the character in an invalid position.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable health, FP, stamina, poise, and status protection before entering a difficult encounter. Damage and one-hit-kill options affect supported attacks but may interfere with scripted boss phases or event triggers. Disable combat modifications before testing normal balance, recording challenge runs, or entering any multiplayer session.
